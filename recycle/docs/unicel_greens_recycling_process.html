5Lab assay – the quality gate

The assay records the mass % of each element in a lot. It drives the expected outputs of Recover, the metal-value estimate and the Certificate of Analysis.

Assay workflow

Draft→Submitted→Approved/Rejected
  • Lab Technician enters results and Submits
  • Recycling Manager Approves or rejects
  • External lab: lab partner and PDF certificate required before approval
  • All-zero results cannot be submitted
  • Approved assays are locked; a corrected one replaces it as current (history kept, note on the lot)

6Stage 3 – Recover (hydrometallurgy)

Black mass is leached and purified into battery-grade salts. Here the expected output comes from the actual assay of the lots consumed, not from fixed BoM figures.

Process (work orders, in this order)

LeachingLeach reactor
FiltrationFilter press
PurificationPurification tanks
Solvent extractionSX circuit · NMC
CrystallisationCrystallizer · NMC
Li precipitationPrecipitation reactor
Drying & packingDryer and packing

Expected output formula

contained metal kg = Σ (input lot kg × element % from assay)
expected metal kg = contained × recovery %
expected product kg = expected metal ÷ metal content % of the salt

Outputs per chemistry

ChemistryMain productBy-products
NMCNickel sulphateCobalt sulphate, Lithium carbonate, Manganese sulphate, Graphite, Sodium sulphate
LFPLithium carbonateIron phosphate, Graphite, Sodium sulphate

7Actual weights – when the yield differs

Recycling yields are never exact. Odoo records what the scale shows; the input stays at what was really consumed, and alerts tell the manager when something is off.

Record Weights screen

Input weighed inOne line per battery lot
↓
Output weighed outMain product (vs planned) + every by-product
↓
Live mass balanceLoss % vs BoM max · Yield % vs accepted range
↓
Confirm & Finish OrderNo backorder or consumption pop-ups on recycling MOs

Scenarios (BoM: 1,000 kg → 650 kg cells, range 58.5 – 71.5 %)

Weighed outYieldScreenResult
650 cells + by-products as planned65 %GreenDone, no alert
600 cells60 %GreenDone; lot holds 600 kg
550 cells55 %OrangeDone; Yield out of range alert to manager
760 cells76 %OrangeDone; yield alert
Output above input (1,060 kg)–RedBlocked – "check the scale"; manager may override

In every case the batteries consumed stay at 1,000 kg. A low yield raises the cost per kg of the cells; a high yield lowers it.

9EPR certificates – second revenue stream

Under the Battery Waste Management Rules 2022, recyclers generate EPR certificates on the CPCB portal for the batteries they process, and sell them to battery producers who must meet recycling targets.

Certificate lifecycle

Draft→Generated→Listed→Sold→Transferred

↓
No double claimingKg can never exceed what the linked MOs still have available
↓
GeneratedCPCB certificate number entered
↓
Create QuotationBuyer + ₹/kg → sale order with the EPR service product
↓
Invoice posted → SoldAutomatic
